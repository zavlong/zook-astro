# Xenobiology, Xenoflora & Xenofungi Ecosystem Skill

Operational guidelines, scientific models, taxonomic tables, and linguistic protocols for designing extraterrestrial autotrophs (Xenoflora) and heterotrophic decomposers (Xenofungi) in Zook Astro.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Exobiological Design

1. **Environmental Determinism**: Organisms are direct evolutionary products of their planetary habitat—surface gravity, atmospheric column density, solvent chemistry, stellar radiation spectrum, and available solid land.
2. **Strict Habitat Grounding & Ecological Biome Correspondence**:
   - **Ecological Regimes**: Biomes are partitioned into distinct ecological envelopes (Cryo/Glacial `[7, 8, 13, 18, 21]`, Volcanic `[14, 15, 22]`, Arid `[9, 10, 11, 16]`, Temperate Land `[3, 4, 5, 6, 12]`, Aquatic `[0, 1, 2, 17, 23]`).
   - **No Cross-Regime Incongruity**: *Never* generate temperate trees or lush forest shrubs on cryogenic nitrogen plains (`18`), ice fissures (`21`), or volcanic lava calderas (`15, 22`).
   - **Cryo Worlds**: On cryogenic or frozen worlds, life must be psychrophilic/cryophilic (e.g. Cryotectonic Ice-Fronds, Nitrogen-Pore Cryo-Lichens, Glacial Sublimation Spires, Sub-Zero Hyphal Nets) utilizing cryo-pyrrole pigments and cryo-proteoglycan/trehalose structural polymers.
   - **Ocean Worlds (0% Solid Land)**: *Never* generate terrestrial trees, shrubs, soil crusts, or forest mushrooms. Only pelagic floating hydrophytes, benthic kelp ribbons, marine snow saprobes, and abyssal hydrothermal vent fungi are permissible.
   - **Desert / Airless Worlds (0% Liquid)**: *Never* generate pelagic or aquatic taxa.
   - **Unicellular Planets (< 2.8 Gyr)**: Replace complex arborescent canopies with biological soil crusts, endolithic biofilms, and microbial algal plains.
3. **Semantic Linguistic Concordance**:
   - Latin/Greek binomial scientific names must **strictly and accurately mirror** English common names in meaning.
   - A species named *Chlorodendron grandis* must be a large green tree, never a small winged purple shrub.
   - Adjectival epithets must grammatically agree in gender with the genus suffix (*masculine*, *feminine*, or *neuter*).
4. **Natural, Non-Formulaic Language**:
   - Never write robotic boilerplate like *"adapted to the planetary conditions of..."*.
   - Describe physiological adaptations, ecological niches, and physical mechanisms directly (e.g., *"Hollow silica-reinforced flutes dissipate severe wind shear on high-pressure plains."*).

## 2. Astrophysical & Biophysical Scaling Equations

### A. Gravitational Scaling (Allometry & Biomass Architecture)
- **Canopy Height ($h$)**: Structural buckling limits scale with gravity $g$ (in Earth gees):
  $$h \propto \left(\frac{1}{g}\right)^{0.65}$$
  - *Low gravity ($g < 0.6$)*: Slender, towering mega-canopies (30–60 m), soaring spire columns, wide-fanning fronds.
  - *Standard gravity ($0.6 \le g \le 1.4$)*: Balanced arborescent forms (10–25 m).
  - *High gravity ($g > 1.4$)*: Squat, buttressed, ground-hugging succulent cushions (< 4 m) with massive structural stipes.
- **Canopy Spread ($w$)**: Lateral branch reach scales as $w \propto (1/g)^{0.55}$.

### B. Atmospheric Pressure ($P_{\text{bar}}$) & Aerodynamics
- **Dense Atmospheres ($P > 2.0\,\text{bar}$)**: High aerodynamic drag; foliage requires streamlined cuticular flutes, perforated blades, or elastic stems. Seeds use buoyant gas micro-balloons.
- **Thin Atmospheres ($P < 0.4\,\text{bar}$)**: Low convective cooling and high evaporation risk; waxy cuticles, succulent water reservoirs, and explosive ballistic spore dehiscence.

### C. Stellar Spectrum ($T_{\text{eff}}$) & Photosynthetic Pigments
Photosynthetic pigments evolve to absorb peak photon flux while reflecting or shielding against damaging wavelengths:
1. **M-Dwarf Cool Stars ($T_{\text{eff}} < 3900\,\text{K}$)**:
   - *Trophic Mechanism*: Near-IR Anoxygenic Bacteriochlorotroph.
   - *Pigment Complex*: Bacteriochlorophyll & Retinal.
   - *Color Palette*: Purple, Obsidian-Black, Deep Burgundy (`#4a1f59`, `#381445`, `#2a0e36`).
2. **K-Dwarf Orange Stars ($3900\,\text{K} \le T_{\text{eff}} < 5100\,\text{K}$)**:
   - *Trophic Mechanism*: Carotenoid-Enhanced Photoautotroph.
   - *Pigment Complex*: Carotenoid & Xanthophyll Antennae.
   - *Color Palette*: Amber, Russet, Golden-Bronze (`#b87b28`, `#995f19`, `#c98a34`).
3. **G-Dwarf Yellow Stars ($5100\,\text{K} \le T_{\text{eff}} \le 6800\,\text{K}$)**:
   - *Trophic Mechanism*: Oxygenic Chlorophyll A/B Photoautotroph.
   - *Pigment Complex*: Chlorophyll A/B Light Harvesting Complex.
   - *Color Palette*: Emerald, Forest, Olive Green (`#3d7a42`, `#28612e`, `#4d9454`).
4. **F/A-Star Hot Bright Stars ($T_{\text{eff}} > 6800\,\text{K}$)**:
   - *Trophic Mechanism*: UV-Shielding Phycocyanin & Anthocyanin Phototroph.
   - *Pigment Complex*: Phycocyanin & Cuticular Melanin Shield.
   - *Color Palette*: Cyan, Cerulean, Teal, Slate Blue (`#237e87`, `#2c939e`, `#38a8b5`).

### D. Solvents & Structural Polymers
- **Water ($\text{H}_2\text{O}$)**: Lignified cellulose, hemicellulose, and chitin-glucan matrices.
- **Ammonia-Water ($\text{NH}_3\text{-H}_2\text{O}$)**: Sub-zero cryo-proteoglycans, glycerol-cellulose hydrogels, and chitosan cold-active gels.
- **Hydrocarbons ($\text{CH}_4 / \text{C}_2\text{H}_6$)**: Silica-sclerotized polyaromatic frustules, polycyclic hydrocarbons, and non-aqueous lipid membranes.

### A. Binomial Construction Rule
Every species name follows the formula:
$$\text{Scientific Name} = \underbrace{\text{[Color Prefix]} + \text{[Form Suffix]}}_{\text{Genus (Capitalized)}} \quad \underbrace{\text{[Specific Epithet]}}_{\text{Species (Lowercase adjectival concordance)}}$$
$$\text{English Name} = \text{[Epithet Translation]} + \text{ } + \text{[Color Translation]} + \text{ } + \text{[Noun Translation]}$$

## 5. Worked Examples of Correct Concordance

- *Chlorodendron grande* $\to$ "Large Emerald Tree-Arbor" (Neuter agreement: *dendron* is neuter $\to$ *grande*).
- *Phosphoroagaricus bioluminescens* $\to$ "Bioluminescent Glowing Gilled-Cap" (Masculine agreement: *agaricus* is masc $\to$ *bioluminescens*).
- *Melanoclavaria volcanica* $\to$ "Volcanic Obsidian Antler-Coral" (Feminine agreement: *clavaria* is fem $\to$ *volcanica*).
- *Cryopulvinus pumilus* $\to$ "Compact Frost Cushion-Dome" (Masculine agreement: *pulvinus* is masc $\to$ *pumilus*).
- *Cyanophykos pelagicum* $\to$ "Marine Azure Marine-Ribbon" (Neuter agreement: *phykos* is neuter $\to$ *pelagicum*).
